## Concept

**E-E-A-T** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) lives in Google’s **Search Quality Rater Guidelines**. It is how humans audit result quality. It is **not** a documented per-page score you can pull from an API, and stuffing “E-E-A-T” in a title does nothing.

Off-page, the useful translation is: **do independent sources treat you as a real organization and real authors, for this topic?**

- **Experience** — first-hand work: original photos, methods, “we measured / we shipped.” Hard to fake off-site without the work existing.
- **Expertise** — credentials and track record others already describe.
- **Authoritativeness** — who cites you, who you are in the field, not who you paid to call you an authority.
- **Trust** — HTTPS, honest WHOIS/business details, real customer service, no scammy ads on the host. For YMYL topics (money, health), the bar is higher (Module 09).

On-page E-E-A-T (byline, policy pages) is a different module. Here: citations, author pages on *other* sites, professional profiles, and not running a link scheme that torches trust.

## Why it matters

A medical or finance query will not be “fixed” by 200 guest posts. Raters — and ranking systems that approximate quality — look for independent corroboration. Off-page spam makes Trust worse even if a backlink graph looks thick.

<CorrectExample>
Author identity is the same person everywhere: site bio, conference talk, and a real publication.

```html
<!-- on your site -->
<a href="https://www.example.com/authors/leila-nouri">Leila Nouri</a>
wrote the protocol.

<!-- on a conference host you do not control -->
<p>
  Speaker: Leila Nouri, Example Labs —
  <a href="https://www.example.com/research/crawl-log-study">crawl-log study</a>

```

Organization JSON-LD `sameAs` matches those profiles. No fake MD credentials.
</CorrectExample>

## WordPress implementation

**Yoast SEO**

1. Yoast → Search Appearance → Schema: `Person` for actual authors with a unique author archive that is not thin. `noindex` empty author archives (Module 03) if they duplicate the blog.
2. Organization knowledge graph: real address/contact if you claim them. Fake NAP is local + trust damage.

**Rank Math**

1. Rank Math author schema / additional profiles (`sameAs` on the user). Link LinkedIn/GitHub that `200`.
2. Do not enable 15 “Expert” schema types on a recipe blog.

**AIOSEO**

1. AIOSEO author SEO / E-E-A-T style modules: fill only true fields. Empty “awards” lists are noise.
2. Multiple authors must not share one ghost byline for every money page.

**Feature gap:** Yoast, Rank Math, AIOSEO, and SEOPress will all emit Person JSON-LD for a fake user. The plugin cannot phone the medical board. The gap is editorial policy, not a missing checkbox.

## Code implementation

```ts
export const personJsonLd = {
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Person",
  name: "Leila Nouri",
  url: "https://www.example.com/authors/leila-nouri",
  jobTitle: "Research engineer",
  worksFor: { "@type": "Organization", name: "Example Labs" },
  sameAs: ["https://github.com/leila-nouri"],
};
```

App Router: author pages as real routes (`app/authors/[slug]/page.tsx`) with HTML bios, not only JSON-LD. Pages Router: `pages/authors/[slug].tsx`. If the Person `url` 404s, the off-page story collapses.

Do not generate 50 author pages from a CSV of names for “E-E-A-T.” That is scaled fakery.

## Common mistakes and outdated myths

- **DEPRECATED:** “E-E-A-T is a ranking factor named in the algorithm notes.” It is a rater concept; systems try to measure quality, not a plugin score.
- **DEPRECATED:** Guest posts on “high DA” blogs as a substitute for expertise.
- Buying awards / “best of” badges that are paid directories.
- Copying another site’s author bio.
- Assuming a Wikipedia page is required. It is neither required nor something you should game.

## Senior / production notes

For YMYL, legal and compliance own claims; SEO owns crawlable truth (who wrote this, who funded it, when it was reviewed). Off-page: prefer citations from institutions in the same specialty over lifestyle blogs.

When an expert leaves the company, do not keep their byline on new articles. Redirect or update the author URL; leaving a ghost is a trust bug.

Agencies: if the client has no named humans, you cannot “implement E-E-A-T” with schema. You can only stop lying.

## How to verify

| Check | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Author URL | `200`, unique bio, matching `sameAs` | Stub archive / 404 |
| Independent citations | Hosts in the actual field | Only sites you paid |
| Claims | Credentials checkable | “Dr.” only in schema |
| GSC / manual actions | No spam / site reputation issues | Money-blog network residue |

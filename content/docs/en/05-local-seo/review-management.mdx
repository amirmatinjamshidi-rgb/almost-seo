## Concept

Reviews on Google (and other surfaces) are **user content** about the location. They affect conversion and feed prominence. They are not a field you type into WordPress.

Google’s review policies (current Business Profile / Maps guidance) forbid:

- Fake reviews, including ones you write, buy, or swap.
- **Incentivized** reviews (discounts for stars) where the policy disallows them.
- Gating: “only happy customers get the Google link; unhappy people get a form.”
- Reviewing your own business or pressuring staff to flood the listing.

Allowed: ask customers **who already interacted** to share feedback, link to the review form without filtering by sentiment, reply publicly without threatening or stuffing keywords.

Stars in schema (`AggregateRating`) must reflect a **visible, real** review system on the page. Fake 4.9 in JSON-LD is spam.

## Why it matters

A location with no reviews and a competitor with a trail of real ones loses the click even at equal distance. A bought-review spike can take the listing down. Reputation is ops plus policy, not a plugin score.

<CorrectExample>
After a paid visit, the receipt or SMS points at the official GBP review URL. No “5 stars only.” Staff replies to criticism with a fix, not with “visit our cheap SEO”:

```txt
Thanks for coming in. If you want to rate the café on Google, here is the listing.
If something went wrong, email hello@example.com — we read that too.
```

On-site testimonials are clearly labeled, dated, and not copied into `AggregateRating` unless you actually display that average.
</CorrectExample>

## WordPress implementation

**Yoast SEO**

1. Yoast does not pull GBP reviews. Do not paste fake `AggregateRating` via a snippet plugin.
2. If you use a testimonial block, it is on-page copy, not Google Maps reviews.

**Rank Math**

1. Rank Math schema → reviews / rating: only if the template **shows** those reviews. Hidden JSON-LD ratings are a classic rich-result spam pattern.
2. Rank Math “FAQ / review” modules on a location page do not replace GBP.

**AIOSEO**

1. AIOSEO Local / schema rating fields: same rule — visible or omit.
2. Do not enable review schema sitewide from a dummy 5-star default.

**Feature gap:** Some WordPress review-slider plugins will emit `AggregateRating` from items you typed. Yoast/Rank Math/AIOSEO/SEOPress will serialize whatever you enter. None verify against Google. The gap is policy, not UI.

## Code implementation

```ts
export const locationJsonLd = {
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "CafeOrCoffeeShop",
  name: "Example Labs Café",
  url: "https://www.example.com/locations/example-st",
  telephone: "+982155550101",
  // omit aggregateRating unless reviews render in HTML
};

export function ReviewAsk() {
  return (
    <p>
      <a href="https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID">
        Review us on Google
      </a>
    </p>
  );
}
```

App Router: Place ID from GBP, not guessed. Pages Router: same link, server-rendered. Do not `useEffect`-swap the href after you detect a low star (gating).

Never fetch a “buy reviews” API. If you show first-party reviews, store them in your DB with dates and render HTML + matching JSON-LD.

## Common mistakes and outdated myths

- **DEPRECATED:** “More reviews always rank you #1 in the pack” as a standalone lever (distance and relevance still exist).
- **DEPRECATED:** Filtering unhappy customers to a Google form and happy ones to Maps.
- Keyword stuffing in owner responses (`best plumber Vanak`).
- Buying reviews on Telegram / freelancer sites.
- Copying competitors’ review text.
- Schema stars without on-page reviews.

## Senior / production notes

Process: train staff on **when** to ask (after service), not on a quota of five-star screenshots. Escalations go to a human, not to a “we will report you” reply on Maps.

Multi-location: each Place ID has its own reviews. Do not paste Branch A’s quotes onto Branch B.

Legal: defamation and takedown have their own paths; fake a review report in GBP when it truly breaks policy, not when it is merely a 2-star.

YMYL locals (clinics, lawyers): incentivized reviews are extra radioactive.

## How to verify

| Check | Pass | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GBP reviews | Real names, mixed dates, mixed stars | Burst of 5-stars same week, same prose |
| Ask flow | Same URL for everyone | Gating by sentiment |
| JSON-LD | No rating, or rating matches visible HTML | 4.9 / thousands, empty page |
| Owner replies | Human, on-topic | Keyword spam |
